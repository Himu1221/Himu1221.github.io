Decisions, with the assumptions visible.

StockWise is an independent portfolio project using reproducible simulated retail data (seed 1221). The snapshot is 31 October 2026. All amounts are CAD; taxes, freight, expiry and reservations are excluded.

How to read the model

  • Demand estimate: sales on days with positive closing stock ÷ those days. Zero-closing days are excluded conservatively. This proxy can still be biased; it is not a forecast or a measured lost-sales figure.
  • Cover: on-hand stock ÷ baseline daily demand. Open orders are excluded from cover and included in inventory position.
  • Reorder trigger: position ≤ demand × (lead time + safety buffer). Target adds the review interval. Order quantities round up to pack multiples.
  • Budget: allocate affordable packs in a fixed baseline cover order. This is a transparent heuristic, not an optimizer. Filters define a separate purchasing scope.
  • Supplier rate: delivered on or before promise ÷ all delivered orders, including any early deliveries. Open overdue orders are separate.
