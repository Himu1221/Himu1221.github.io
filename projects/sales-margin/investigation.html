Interpretation and next steps

Gross loss sums only negative observations; net profit includes positive offsets. Loss exposure is not guaranteed savings. Cost allocation, contracts, customer mix, and currency require validation. The proposed next step is a focused finance/sales review, followed by an approved limited test with agreed margin, profit, volume, and sales guardrails.

Compare equal months only; equal coverage does not control for business mix. Profit percentage growth is omitted when a negative baseline would mislead. Monthly movement is not seasonally adjusted. Cross-segment comparisons are not matched controls.
